# TIV Security Policy & Vulnerability Disclosure

Tonmoy Infrastructure and Vision (TIV) values the contribution of independent security researchers. We are committed to maintaining the integrity, confidentiality, and reliability of our open-source software and infrastructure systems.

---

## 1. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability in any TIV system, software repository, or infrastructure service, please report it immediately:

- **Email**: `security@tonmoyinfrastructure.org`
- **Contact Form**: [https://tonmoyinfrastructure.org/contact](https://tonmoyinfrastructure.org/contact) (Category: Security)
- **Security Center**: [https://tonmoyinfrastructure.org/security](https://tonmoyinfrastructure.org/security)

Please encrypt your message using our PGP public key whenever submitting sensitive technical details or proof-of-concept exploits.

## 3. Response Commitments & SLA

- **Initial Acknowledgment**: Within 48 hours of report receipt.
- **Triage & Severity Classification**: Within 5 business days.
- **Status Updates**: Bi-weekly updates until fix is validated and released.
- **Public Disclosure**: Coordinated disclosure after 90 days or upon patch availability.

---

## 4. Safe Harbor Commitment

TIV supports responsible security research conducted in good faith. If you:
1. Make a reasonable effort to avoid privacy violations, degradation of user experience, or disruption to production services;
2. Do not exploit a security issue beyond the minimum extent necessary to demonstrate its presence;
3. Give us a reasonable time to resolve the issue before disclosing it publicly;

TIV will not pursue legal action against you or request law enforcement investigation regarding your research.
